## Tutorial: Your first board powered by an RP2040
In this tutorial, we will create a simple board powered by an RP2040 microcontroller.

To follow this guide, you don't need any experience or even basic knowledge of hardware development; all you need is curiosity and a willingness to learn by doing!

To make the tutorial easier and more accessible to those who already have some knowledge of the subject, it will be divided into chapters and sections, each of which will cover a different part of the board creation process.
