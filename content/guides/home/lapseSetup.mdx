## Setup Lapse
To track the time spent on your projects, you can use Lapse: a simple and efficient time tracking tool that records the time you spend on different tasks and projects.
Lapse is free and open source, and it can be easily installed on your computer.

## Use Lapse
Lapse is a web-based application, so you don't need to install anything on your computer.
You can access it from any web browser, and it will automatically save your data in the cloud.

To setup Lapse, visit the official website: [https://lapse.hackclub.com/](https://lapse.hackclub.com/).

Here you have to login with your Hack Club account.

## Start a new Time Lapse
Once you are logged in, you can start a new time lapse by clicking on the "Create" button.
This will open a new window where you can select what windows you want to track, like in a presentation on Google Meet.
After selecting the windows you want to track, click on the "Start" button to begin tracking your time.

You can pause and stop the time tracking at any time by clicking on the "Pause" and "Stop" buttons, respectively.

After you stop the time tracking, you can view the recorded time lapse and see how much time you spent on each task and project.

For each hour spent, the resoulting video will be 1 minute long, so you can easily see how much time you spent on each task and project by watching the video.

Before to publish your time Lapse, you can edit the title and description of your activity.
If you want, you can also cut the video to remove any unnecessary parts or to highlight specific moments.
