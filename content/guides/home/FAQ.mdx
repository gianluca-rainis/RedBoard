### What are the requirements?
The shipping process is split in 2 phases:

#### Phase 1: Board design
* Create a board design (we recommend KiCad) and track the time spent with Lapse.
* The board design must be open source and shared on GitHub.
* The board design must be functional and manufacturable.
* The GitHub repo must include an handwritten README file that presents the board and the design.
* The GitHub repo must include a JOURNEY file that documents the process of designing the board, the challenges faced, and how you overcame them. This is a very important part of the process, as it allows us to understand your design process and learn from it.
* The GitHub repo must include a detailed bill of materials (BOM) with links to the components used in the design.
* Share some pictures of the board's final design.

#### Phase 2: Board manufacturing
* Manufacture the board you designed in phase 1 and track the time spent with Lapse.
* Make a short video showing the board working.
* Share some pictures of the manufactured board.

### How is scoring determined?
For both the phases the board, once the shipment is approved, you will receive a number of credits based on the time you tracked with Lapse and the quality of your board.
The quality of the board is determined by a grading system that takes into account the functionality, manufacturability, and documentation of the board.
For having the shipment approved you must follow the requirements of each phase and submit your board for review.

### How do you earn Credits?
For every hour of tracked work, you will earn a base of 1 credit.
For each project, you will have two payouts:
* The first payout is for the design phase, and it is based on the time spent on KiCad.
* The second payout is for the manufacturing phase, and it is based on the time spent building the board and making it work.

---
### Can I use AI?
You can use AI to help you, as long as you declare what you use it on your Project's AI statement section.
However you can NOT vibe code the entire firmware, or use AI to design the entire board.
We want to see your creativity and your design process, not just an AI's one.
